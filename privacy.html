Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Who we are

Moment Clips is a personal, non-commercial tool run by its developer. Contact: github.com/kravchenski.

What data is used

  • The OAuth access token and refresh token that TikTok issues when the account owner authorises the app, and the account's display name and posting options returned by the TikTok API.
  • The videos, titles and hashtags that the owner uploads through the app.

No data about other TikTok users is collected.

How it is used and stored

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own TikTok account. Tokens are kept in a private file on the developer's own computer or server. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes access. Revoking access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services) invalidates them, and the stored file can be deleted at any time. To ask for deletion, write to github.com/kravchenski.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and has no public sign-up.
