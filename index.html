Moment Clips

Moment Clips is a personal, non-commercial tool. It cuts long videos into short vertical clips and uploads the finished clips to the TikTok account of its owner, after the owner has authorised the app with TikTok Login.

It does not collect data about other TikTok users and has no public sign-up.

Connect your TikTok account

The owner signs in with TikTok and authorises two permissions: user.info.basic (shows which account is connected) and video.upload (sends a finished clip to the account's drafts; the owner writes the caption, chooses who can watch and posts it in the TikTok app).
